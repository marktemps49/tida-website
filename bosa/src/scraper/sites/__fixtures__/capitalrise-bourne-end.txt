Investment Summary
PLAN – CapitalRise is providing a bridge loan to the Borrower to refinance the existing debt secured on a development of six houses in Bourne End, Buckinghamshire (the Properties) and provide a sales bridge period. CapitalRise investors will fund the loan alongside an institutional funding partner. Investors will be repaid after the institutional funding partner and will receive a higher rate of return to reflect the increased risk. N.B. There are no phases or tiers to this deal, and this represents the sole opportunity for CapitalRise investors to invest in this deal.
TERM – The estimated term for this investment is 12-18 months. If the loan is not repaid by the end of the estimated term, returns will accrue on investments for a further 6 months from the end of the estimated term up until the hard stop date (August 2028).
LOCATION – The Properties are in Bourne End, Buckinghamshire, a county which forms part of the London Commuter belt.  The Properties are between the M40 to the north and the M4 to the south with good accessibility to both. Bourne End has a direct rail link to London and is approximately five miles south-east of High Wycombe and three miles east of Marlow, which provides the main retail and leisure facilities.
PROPERTY - The Properties are six substantial detached houses, which have already been constructed. Each house is individually designed with at least five bedrooms, with one house having a triple garage. Each house has been built to a high specification and finish. Internally, the Properties have wood effect luxury vinyl tiles throughout the ground floor parts and carpet to the stairs and bedrooms and fully-tiled bathrooms and WCs. The Properties are listed with Savills and Bovingdons, which are both established and experienced agents in this area. You can see the link to the Savills sales brochure here
THE BORROWER – The ultimate beneficiary owner (UBO) of the borrowing entity is an experienced property professional who has over a decade of experience delivering high specification residential developments across London and the Home Counties
THE SECURITY – The security package for the loan to the Borrower is shared with the institutional funding partner and includes Shared First Legal Charge over the Properties. CapitalRise investors are subordinated to the institutional funding partner and will be repaid after the institutional funding partner. Therefore, this investment has a risk profile more like a mezzanine loan than a senior loan and CapitalRise investors will receive a higher rate of return than the institutional funding partner to reflect the increased risk.
SECURITY TRUSTEE - CapitalRise is the security trustee for both CapitalRise investors and the institutional funding partner. CapitalRise will always act in the best interests of both CapitalRise investors and the institutional funding partner and will always seek to recover both invested capital and accrued interest in full. If the Borrower were unable to repay the loan, CapitalRise could force the sale of the Property and would always seek to recover all invested capital and accrued returns on behalf of all investors. In this situation, CapitalRise investors will be paid after the institutional funding partner but before the Borrower.
VALUATION - Bruton Knowles have provided an independent Red Book market valuation (July 2026). It provides a current market value (CMV) of the Properties of £20.0m (32,904 sq.ft). CapitalRise investors' gross breakeven is £456 psf
LOAN TO VALUE – The day one loan to value (LTV) on this investment is 68% based on the CMV of £20.0m. The exit LTV for CapitalRise investors is anticipated to be 75% based on the CMV of £20.0m.
EXIT PLAN – The Borrower plans to exit the loan through the sale of the Properties, which will allow them to pay back the CapitalRise debt. If a sale is not completed within the term, the Borrower may seek to refinance the CapitalRise debt. It should be noted that as the Properties are being sold as independent units it is likely that there will be partial redemptions on this opportunity.

Investment Structure
A subsidiary company of CapitalRise, CR BOURNE END SS LIMITED has been incorporated to issue bonds to investors. Corporate information on CR BOURNE END SS LIMITED can be found at Companies House here.
CR BOURNE END SS LIMITED has already sourced some finance from pre-funding investors in order to facilitate the property transaction. We, CapitalRise Finance Limited, are one of the pre-funders and the other pre-funders may be associated with us. The CapitalRise investors' funds will be used to refinance the pre-funders.
As this phase has been pre-funded, settlement of investments is not conditional on reaching a minimal threshold of funding.
Your investment is ring-fenced from other CapitalRise investments because CR BOURNE END SS LIMITED will not carry on any other commercial activity. The directors of this company will (through CapitalRise) administer and enforce the terms of the bonds issued by CR BOURNE END SS LIMITED.
The investment is structured as deep-discounted bonds. Deep-discounted bonds (also known as a zero coupon bond where no interest is paid or discount bond) are bonds bought at a price lower than its face value, with the face value repaid at the time of maturity.
Investors in all phases are ranked pari passu (equal). This means holders of earlier issued bonds will be repaid at the same time as holders of bonds issued during later phases.
CR BOURNE END SS LIMITED is the beneficiary of a Shared First Legal Charge. In any forced sale of the Property, investors will recover their capital and accrued return before the Borrower, but after the institutional funding partner. Due to the higher risk, investors will benefit from a higher rate of return than the institutional funding partner.
If the bonds are repaid before the hard stop date then CR BOURNE END SS LIMITED is not required to withhold UK income tax on this investment and so returns will be paid gross.
Should the bonds be repaid after the hard stop date (August 2028) late payment interest will accrue at the rate of 12.20% p.a. For bonds not purchased using the CapitalRise IFISA which have accrued interest after the hard stop date, CapitalRise is required by HMRC to withhold the basic rate of tax (20%) on interest payments and pay this directly to HMRC. If the basic rate is not applicable, you may need to contact HMRC. If you are not a UK taxpayer you will need to reclaim this tax payment from HMRC. Income tax may be payable by investors on any return on the investments unless you invest via your CapitalRise IFISA. Investors should take their own tax advice from an appropriately qualified professional.

Risks
As with all investments you should carefully consider the key risks involved which include:

Risks | How this applies to you
Capital and Income Risk – Your capital is at risk and the income is not guaranteed. | CapitalRise will typically take a first or second legal charge over the Properties on your behalf. On this investment, CR BOURNE END SS LIMITED is funding alongside an institutional funding partner who will be repaid first. They both have the benefit of a shared first legal charge against the Property. This means the Borrower cannot sell the asset without first repaying the loan or allocating proceeds from the sale to repay it. A legal charge does not guarantee the repayment of your investment. In any forced sale of the Properties, investors will recover their capital and accrued return before the Borrower. CapitalRise Investors are subordinated to the institutional funding partner and will be repaid after the institutional funding partner has been repaid in full.
Compensation Scheme – The Financial Services Compensation Scheme does not cover poor investment performance. | You cannot claim FSCS compensation if your original investment or returns are lost.
Subordination Risk – Investors will be repaid after the institutional funding partner | Whilst you have access to a shared first legal charge, for the avoidance of doubt, this investment is not like a senior loan because you will be repaid after the institutional funding partner which means that this investment has a risk profile more similar to a mezzanine loan than a senior loan which is reflected in the higher rate of return for investors.
Market Risk – If the market for residential property in the local area deteriorates, it may not be possible to sell the Properties at the price currently projected and/or there could be a delay in the sales. | If sales of the Properties are achieved the value would need to be less than 75% of the current market value (CMV) before investors' invested capital and accrued return are at risk.
Investment Liquidity Risk – You need to be prepared to hold this investment for the full term. | We do operate the CapitalRise Bulletin Board where you can post your investment for sale at its current value. This does not guarantee a sale. CapitalRise will charge a fee of 1.5% of the sale if a buyer is found. You can post your investment for sale from within your CapitalRise account. Investments can be posted at any point up until one month prior to the start of the original range of the estimated end date (in this case 30th June 2027).
Recovery Risk - If CapitalRise has to enforce its security package the enforcement of security may not be sufficient to cover the return of all capital and interest | Enforcing security is time-consuming and carries additional costs. If the Borrower defaults and CapitalRise has to enforce the security package, there is a risk that the amount that can be recovered is less than the amount that is due to investors and investors may receive back only a proportion of their original capital investment or none at all.
Sales Rate Risk – Sales may be slower than forecast and/or the properties may sell for less | The primary exit for your investment is through sales of the Properties. It is important to note that at least 5 of the 6 Properties would need to be sold to repay the bonds in full. Your investment includes a 6-month buffer from the end of the estimated repayment date up until the hard stop date of August 2028 in the event that potential sales take longer than anticipated.
Refinance Risk - The Borrower may refinance their debt at the end of the term. They may not be able to do so | One potential exit for your investment is to refinance with another lender. This may take longer than planned or not be possible at all. In a refinance, a lender will only lend up to a percentage of the value of the Properties, which may not be sufficient to repay the whole of the CapitalRise loan. Your investment has an estimated term of 12-18 months and a 6-month buffer from the end of the estimated repayment date until the hard stop date (August 2028) in the event that a refinance takes longer than anticipated.
